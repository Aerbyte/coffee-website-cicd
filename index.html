Our Story

Born from a love of honest coffee.

Ember & Oak started as a small corner shop with a simple belief: great coffee shouldn't be complicated, just intentional. We built relationships with farmers in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — paying fair prices, visiting farms, and letting the beans speak for themselves.


Our roastery sits in the back of the café. When you walk in, you'll smell exactly what we're crafting that day. Nothing here is mass-produced, nothing pre-packaged. Just coffee, done right.
